A City of Its Own

Greenwood Village is its own city of roughly 15,000 people across about 8 square miles in Arapahoe County. The Denver Tech Center sits inside its limits, which is why many residents have short commutes.

The Outdoors

The city maintains more than 180 acres of parks and about 276 acres of open space, including Westlands Park and the Marjorie Perry Nature Preserve. The High Line Canal Trail winds through on a natural-surface path, and there are equestrian trails and parks too.

The Homes

Housing ranges from gated estates on multi-acre lots to mid-century ranches and newer custom homes, with condos and townhomes near the Tech Center and its light rail stations.

Schools

Most of Greenwood Village is in the Cherry Creek School District, including Cherry Creek High School. Some neighborhoods are served by Littleton Public Schools depending on which side of Belleview Avenue they're on, so check by address.

Questions Buyers Ask

Greenwood Village FAQ

Is Greenwood Village Part of Denver?

No. Greenwood Village is a separate city in Arapahoe County, just south of Denver along I-25. It's about 20 to 30 minutes from downtown Denver by car.

What School District Is Greenwood Village In?

Most of Greenwood Village is in the Cherry Creek School District. Some neighborhoods are served by Littleton Public Schools depending on which side of Belleview Avenue they're on, so always confirm by address.

Is Greenwood Village a Good Place to Live?

For families who want space, well-regarded schools and a short commute to the Tech Center, it's one of the metro's most popular choices. It's quieter and more car-dependent than central Denver.

Is Every Greenwood Village Address in City Limits?

No. Some pockets with Greenwood Village mailing addresses are unincorporated Arapahoe County, with different taxes and services. Check before you buy.
