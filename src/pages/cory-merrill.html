Life Here

A Family Neighborhood Close to Everything

The Name

The neighborhood is named for its two Denver public schools, Cory Elementary and Merrill Middle School. For many buyers, those schools are the reason to look here.

The Homes

Many of the original homes were small single-story houses built for returning World War II veterans. A lot of them have been replaced by large contemporary homes, while brick ranches and bungalows remain on many blocks.

Shops and Food

Cory-Merrill is mostly residential. There are a few restaurants and shops along its edges, and Cherry Creek's shopping is a short drive north.

Getting Around

Washington Park is just to the west. I-25 and Colorado Boulevard put downtown, Cherry Creek and the Tech Center within easy reach.

Questions Buyers Ask

Cory-Merrill FAQ

Why Is It Called Cory-Merrill?

It's named for Cory Elementary and Merrill Middle School, the two Denver public schools in the neighborhood.

Is Cory-Merrill Good for Families?

It's one of the more family-oriented neighborhoods in central Denver, with neighborhood schools, quiet streets and Washington Park nearby. It's mostly residential, so you'll drive for most errands.

What Kind of Homes Are in Cory-Merrill?

A mix of post-war ranches and bungalows and large new custom homes. Many original homes have been replaced, so new construction is easier to find here than in neighborhoods like Platt Park or Washington Park.

Which Schools Serve Cory-Merrill?

Cory Elementary and Merrill Middle School are in the neighborhood. School boundaries vary by address, so confirm a specific home's assigned schools with Denver Public Schools.
