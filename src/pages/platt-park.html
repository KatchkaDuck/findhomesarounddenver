Life Here

Life Revolves Around South Pearl Street

South Pearl Street

A few blocks of independent restaurants, coffee shops, bars and small stores run through the middle of the neighborhood. On Sundays the South Pearl Street Farmers Market takes over the street, and the neighborhood hosts seasonal events like Oktoberfest and Winterfest.

The Park and Library

James H. Platt Park, at South Pearl Street and Florida Avenue, gives the neighborhood its name. It holds a recreation center, a playground and the Decker Branch Library, a 1913 Carnegie library that is still in use.

The Homes

Most homes date from about 1900 to 1930: Craftsman bungalows, brick Denver Squares, Victorian gable-fronts and a few Spanish Revivals. Modern infill homes and duplexes with rooftop decks sit between them on many blocks.

Getting Around

The Louisiana–Pearl light rail station sits just north of the neighborhood, a short ride from downtown. I-25 is a few blocks north, and Washington Park is a few blocks east.

Why People Love It

  • South Pearl Street within walking distance
  • Light rail to downtown without a car
  • Character homes on tree-lined blocks
  • Washington Park and Old South Gaylord nearby

What to Know First

  • Lots are often narrow, and garages are usually detached and off the alley
  • Older homes need a careful sewer scope and electrical check
  • Street parking near Pearl gets tight during events
  • A new build and an original bungalow can sell far apart on the same block

Questions Buyers Ask

Platt Park FAQ

Is Platt Park a Good Place to Live?

For buyers who want a walkable neighborhood with a real main street, yes. South Pearl Street, the light rail and Washington Park are all close. The trade-offs are smaller lots, older homes that need careful inspections, and event-day crowds near Pearl.

Where Is Platt Park in Denver?

Platt Park is in south-central Denver, roughly between Broadway and Downing Street from Mississippi Avenue south to Evans Avenue. Washington Park is just to the northeast.

Is It Platt Park or Platte Park?

Platt Park. The neighborhood is named for James H. Platt Park at its center. It's often misspelled Platte, after the river and the street, which are in other parts of the city.

Which Schools Serve Platt Park?

Denver Public Schools serving the area include McKinley-Thatcher Elementary, Grant Beacon Middle School and South High School. Boundaries vary by address, so always confirm a specific home's assigned schools with DPS.
