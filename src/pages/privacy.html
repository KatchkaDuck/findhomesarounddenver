Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us through the forms on this site:

  • Home value requests: your name, email, optional phone number, property address and selling timeline.
  • Contact requests: your name, email, optional phone number, what you're interested in and your message.
  • Guide downloads: your name and email, and which guide you downloaded.

Our hosting provider also records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site. We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, as described below. We don't use advertising trackers or sell any data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request, such as preparing a home valuation or answering a question.
  • To follow up by email, phone or text about your real estate plans, if you've given us that contact information.
  • To send occasional market updates or helpful information. You can opt out at any time.

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it with a lender, title company or other service provider only when you ask us to connect you, or when it's needed to complete a transaction you're part of.

Service Providers

This site is hosted by Netlify, which processes form submissions and delivers them to us by email. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers. We use Google Analytics to measure site traffic, such as which pages are visited, how visitors found the site, the general location and type of device, and whether a form was submitted. Google Analytics doesn't receive the names, emails or other details you type into our forms. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies; you can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell new visitors from returning ones and to measure how the site is used. We don't use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics on all sites with Google's browser add-on.
