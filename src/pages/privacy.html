Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us through the forms on this site:

  • Home value requests: your name, email, optional phone number, property address and selling timeline.
  • Contact requests: your name, email, optional phone number, what you're interested in and your message.

Our hosting provider also records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site. We don't use advertising trackers or sell any data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request, such as preparing a home valuation or answering a question.
  • To follow up by email, phone or text about your real estate plans, if you've given us that contact information.
  • To send occasional market updates or helpful information. You can opt out at any time.

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it with a lender, title company or other service provider only when you ask us to connect you, or when it's needed to complete a transaction you're part of.
