Life Here

City Living With a Lot of History

A Neighborhood of Mansions and Apartments

Denver's wealthy families built mansions here in the late 1800s. After the Silver Crash of 1893, building shifted to apartments, and historic buildings like the Colonnade, Alta Court and the Hamilton still stand. Many mansions are now offices or condos.

Landmarks

The Colorado State Capitol, the Molly Brown House Museum, the Governor's Residence and the Cathedral of St. John in the Wilderness are all here. Cheesman Park sits just east, and many residents consider it part of the neighborhood.

The Homes

Most homes are condos and apartments in buildings from the early 1900s, with some townhomes and newer condo buildings. Capitol Hill is Denver's most densely populated neighborhood, with about 16,700 residents in 2020.

Getting Around

Almost everything is walkable. Colfax Avenue on the north edge has frequent buses, restaurants and music venues, and downtown is a short walk or bike ride west.

Why People Love It

  • One of Denver's most affordable ways into homeownership
  • Walk to restaurants, parks, downtown and Cheesman Park
  • Historic buildings with real character
  • Strong rental demand for future landlords

What to Know First

  • Many older buildings have no off-street parking
  • HOA dues, reserves and special assessments vary widely
  • Some buildings aren't approved for FHA or VA loans
  • Colfax brings nightlife noise to the north edge

Questions Buyers Ask

Capitol Hill FAQ

Is Capitol Hill a Good Place to Buy a Condo?

For first-time buyers and downsizers who want to walk everywhere, it's one of Denver's best options. The key is the building: review the HOA's budget, reserves, insurance and any planned assessments before you commit.

What Should I Check Before Buying a Condo in Denver?

Ask for the HOA's budget, reserve study, meeting minutes, insurance and rules on rentals and pets. Find out whether any special assessments are planned, whether parking is deeded or assigned, and whether the building is approved for FHA or VA loans if you need one.

Is Cheesman Park Part of Capitol Hill?

Officially, no. Denver treats Cheesman Park as its own neighborhood just east of Capitol Hill, but many residents consider the two one area.

Where Is Capitol Hill in Denver?

Capitol Hill sits just east of downtown, bounded by Broadway on the west, Downing Street on the east, 7th Avenue on the south and Colfax Avenue on the north.
