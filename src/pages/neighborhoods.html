Questions Buyers Ask

Denver Neighborhood FAQ

What Are the Best Neighborhoods in Denver for Families?

Washington Park, Bonnie Brae, Cory-Merrill and South Park Hill are popular with families for their parks, quiet streets and neighborhood schools. Greenwood Village, just south of Denver, draws families who want larger lots and the Cherry Creek School District. The right fit depends on your budget, commute and the schools assigned to a specific address.

Which Denver Neighborhoods Are the Most Walkable?

Capitol Hill is the most walkable, with almost everything within a few blocks. Platt Park and Washington Park are close behind, thanks to South Pearl Street and Old South Gaylord Street.

Where Can I Find New Construction in Central Denver?

Cory-Merrill and Sloan Lake have more new homes than most central neighborhoods, because many original homes have been replaced. Washington Park and Belcaro also have new custom homes, usually at the top of the price range.

What's a Good First Neighborhood for a Condo or Townhome?

Capitol Hill has the widest choice of condos and townhomes in historic buildings, and it's walkable to downtown. See the first-time buyer guide for what to check in a condo building before you buy.
